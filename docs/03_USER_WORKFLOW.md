# AI Procurement Copilot v1.2 — User Workflow

## Purpose

This document explains the intended sourcing-review journey. It describes decision support, not autonomous execution.

## Step 1 — Establish the decision context

Select the procurement category, commodity, annual volume, display currency and relevant scenario assumptions. Review the visible category profile and unit basis.

## Step 2 — Load supplier information

Use the synthetic demonstration dataset or upload a supported RFQ file. Public demonstrations should use synthetic or sanitized data.

## Step 3 — Validate before comparison

The workflow checks structural requirements, required fields, duplicates, invalid values, currencies, units, capacity and other business rules. Original and normalized data remain distinguishable.

## Step 4 — Review comparison readiness

The application identifies whether the data is eligible, eligible with conditions or blocked. Unsupported or insufficient evidence can prevent final award-oriented language.

## Step 5 — Compare suppliers

Review supplier pricing, total cost, risk, performance, ESG and category-specific factors. The application distinguishes lowest quoted price from broader best-value considerations.

## Step 6 — Test sourcing options

Use scenario, allocation and negotiation views to evaluate concentration, backup supply, cost movement and commercial leverage.

## Step 7 — Review intelligence and evidence

Procurement Intelligence summarizes the sourcing decision context. Supplier Intelligence provides Supplier 360, performance, financial, ESG, innovation and SRM views, subject to evidence limitations.

## Step 8 — Generate outputs

Review the executive memo, supplier clarification communication and explainability content. Download business-readable reports or separate machine-readable audit outputs.

## Step 9 — Human procurement action

A procurement professional validates assumptions, performs due diligence, aligns stakeholders and completes approval outside the application. The application does not execute awards or write to ERP systems.

## ERP Upload Preview

The separate ERP page accepts one XLSX workbook for package-safety and structural preview. It can show sheet names, headers, dimensions and draft static mappings. It does not normalize business records, connect to SAP or Oracle, or feed procurement engines.
