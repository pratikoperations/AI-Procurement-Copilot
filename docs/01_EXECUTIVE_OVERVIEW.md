# AI Procurement Copilot v1.2 — Executive Overview

## What it is

AI Procurement Copilot is a governed procurement decision-support portfolio application for RFQ comparison and sourcing evaluation. It helps procurement professionals review supplier quotations beyond lowest price by making cost, risk, delivery, quality, commercial terms and evidence quality visible.

## Target users

- Procurement managers
- Category managers
- Strategic sourcing leads
- Procurement excellence teams
- Supplier relationship managers

## Three core capabilities

1. **Governed supplier comparison** — validates quotation data and compares suppliers using visible category, currency, unit, cost and risk assumptions.
2. **Sourcing decision support** — supports scenarios, allocation, negotiation preparation and evidence-gated recommendation language.
3. **Executive communication** — produces business-readable memos, supplier clarification content and controlled downloads.

## Business relevance

The project demonstrates how fragmented spreadsheet analysis can be converted into a repeatable, explainable procurement workflow while preserving human approval. It is relevant to strategic sourcing, category management, supplier risk, procurement analytics and digital transformation.
